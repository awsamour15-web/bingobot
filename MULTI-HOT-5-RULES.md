# Multi Hot 5 – Game Rules & Mechanics

## Overview
Multi Hot 5 is a classic 3×3 slots game with 5 fixed paylines, a multiplier reel, and a gamble feature. Players spin to match symbols across paylines and can use the multiplier reel to increase their winnings.

---

## Game Layout

### Grid Structure
- **3 columns × 3 rows** (9 symbol positions total)
- **Multiplier reel** on the left side with options: 1x, 2x, 5x
- **5 fixed paylines** that determine winning combinations

### Symbol Payouts (Per 3-of-a-Kind Match, at 5 ETB bet)
| Symbol | Payout Multiplier | Payout @ 5 ETB | Appearance |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|-----------|
| 🔴 Seven (77) | 15× bet | 75.00 ETB | Red/gold 77 |
| 💰 Double Dollar ($$) | 10× bet | 50.00 ETB | Gold currency symbol |
| 🔔 Bell | 5× bet | 25.00 ETB | Yellow bell |
| 🍉 Watermelon / 🍇 Grape | 4× bet | 20.00 ETB | Watermelon + Grape |
| 🍊 Orange / 🫐 Plum / 🍒 Cherry | 2× bet | 10.00 ETB | Fruit mix group |

> All symbol wins are displayed in ETB.

---

## Payline System

### The 5 Fixed Paylines
Each payline spans left to right across the 3 columns and specifies which row in each column to evaluate:

1. **Middle line** (row 1-1-1) – Center row across all columns
2. **Top line** (row 0-0-0) – Top row across all columns
3. **Bottom line** (row 2-2-2) – Bottom row across all columns
4. **Diagonal ↘** (row 0-1-2) – Top-left to bottom-right
5. **Diagonal ↖** (row 2-1-0) – Bottom-left to top-right

### Win Conditions
A payline wins when the same symbol appears on all three positions in that payline. Multiple paylines can win on a single spin, and their payouts are **combined**.

---

## Multiplier Reel Mechanics

### How It Works
The multiplier reel is a separate spin column on the left side that generates a multiplier value.

### Multiplier Reel Values
- **1x** – No multiplier (standard payout)
- **2x** – Doubles all winning payline payouts
- **5x** – Quintuples all winning payline payouts

### Payout Formula
```
Payout = Bet Amount × Symbol Multiplier × Multiplier Reel Value
```

**Example:**
- Bet: 5 ETB
- Three Sevens on middle line (symbol payout = 15×)
- Multiplier reel shows: 2×
- **Result:** 5 × 15 × 2 = **150 ETB**

### Multiple Paylines with Multiplier
The multiplier reel value is applied to each winning payline independently, then totals are combined.

---

## Gamble Feature (X2 Button)

### Activation
- Available after any winning spin (totalWin > 0)
- Disabled during spins or when there is no active win

### How to Play
1. After a winning spin, click the **X2** button
2. A card appears face-down in the middle of the screen, indicating the gamble feature is active
3. Choose **RED** or **BLACK**
4. The card is revealed:
   - **Correct guess:** Winnings are **doubled**
   - **Incorrect guess:** **Lose all winnings**

### Take Win Option
- Players can click **"Take Win"** at any point to collect their current winnings and add them to the main balance without gambling

---

## House Edge & Payouts

### House Edge
- Default house edge: **35%** (players receive ~65% RTP on average)
- Applied probabilistically on winning spins

### Maximum Win Cap
- **Maximum single spin win:** 20× the bet amount
- Individual payline payouts are reduced proportionally if the cap is exceeded

---

## Spin Mechanics

### Bet Selection
- Bet tiers available, selectable with **-** and **+** buttons
- Paytable shown in-game reflects the currently selected bet amount

### Auto-Spin
- **Auto toggle** enables continuous spinning
- Stops automatically on a win, insufficient balance, or manual toggle

## Summary

Multi Hot 5 combines:
- Classic 3×3 slots layout with 5 fixed paylines
- Multiplier reel (1x, 2x, 5x) for amplified payouts
- Optional X2 gamble feature for risk-reward gameplay
- Auto-spin for continuous play
- ~65% RTP with a 35% house edge
